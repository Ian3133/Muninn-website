# Muninn News Model

Muninn connects published reporting to the real-world activity it describes. The language below keeps broad subjects, evolving contexts, concrete occurrences, and dated changes distinct.

## Language

**Topic**:
A broad, durable subject used to classify coverage across otherwise separate Situations, Events, and Reports.
_Avoid_: Category as a real-world container

**Situation**:
A bounded, evolving real-world context that is understood through multiple distinct Events.
_Avoid_: Storyline, root Event, coverage collection

**Event**:
One coherent real-world occurrence, episode, campaign, or process with its own timeline and outcome.
_Avoid_: Story, Situation, article cluster

**Development**:
A dated change that advances or alters an existing Event without becoming a separate Event itself.
_Avoid_: Event update, Story

**Report**:
A published account that explains one or more Developments and may connect them to an Event or Situation.
_Avoid_: Event, Development
